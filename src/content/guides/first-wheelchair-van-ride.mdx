If you're reading this, you've probably just had the conversation. Mom can't get into the car anymore. Dad's chair doesn't fold. The appointment is next week and you're not sure how to get them there.

Here's what a wheelchair van ride looks like, start to finish, so you both know what's coming.

## Before the ride

When you [book a ride](/book), we ask for the pickup address, where you're going, the date and time, and how your parent gets around. That's it. We don't ask about diagnoses or medical history.

The day before, we call to confirm the pickup time. If your parent has an early appointment, we plan to arrive ten minutes early. That call is also a good time to tell us about anything at the house: a gate code, a steep driveway, a dog who needs to be put up.

## At the door

The driver comes to the front door, not the curb. They'll introduce themselves by name, ask how your parent likes to be helped, and go at their pace.

If your parent uses their own wheelchair, they stay in it. The driver rolls them up the ramp, into the van, and locks the chair to the floor with four straps. Then a lap belt and a shoulder belt, the same as any car seat belt. It takes about two minutes and your parent never has to stand up.

If your parent uses a walker or can walk with an arm, the driver helps them up the step and into a regular seat.

## In the van

The ride is quiet. Most of our drivers keep the radio off unless asked. Your parent can bring a bag, a jacket, a portable oxygen tank, or a companion. Up to two people can ride along.

If you'd like a heads-up, we text you when the driver is on the way and again when your parent is dropped off.

## At the appointment

This is the part families worry about most. The driver doesn't drop your parent at the curb. They walk them inside, to the right floor and the right suite, and make sure they're checked in.

If you booked a wait-and-return ride, the driver stays. When the appointment is over, the front desk calls and the driver is back at the suite door within a few minutes. Then the same careful ride home.

## What it costs

You'll know the price before the ride. We tell you when we confirm, and it doesn't change after. Our [pricing page](/pricing) lists the rules in plain words.

## One more thing

The first ride is the hardest one. After that, your parent knows the driver's name and the driver knows how they take their coffee. That's the whole point.
