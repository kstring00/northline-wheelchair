The nurse just said "we're planning to discharge tomorrow." It's good news. It also means you have about a day to arrange a ride for someone who can't get into your car.

Here's how to do it without stress.

## Ask the nurse three things

1. **Roughly what time?** Discharges often slip to the afternoon. Ask for a window, not a time.
2. **Which entrance?** Most hospitals bring patients to the main lobby, but some use a separate discharge door.
3. **Will they need a wheelchair for the ride, or can they walk with help?** The answer changes which van we send.

## Book the ride today

Don't wait for the final word. [Request a ride](/book?for=loved-one) or [call us](tel:+12815550142) now with the window the nurse gave you. We'll hold the spot and adjust the time tomorrow when the paperwork is done. Same-day changes on discharges are normal for us.

If the hospital is one we drive to every week, we already know where to pull in. Our [hospital pages](/service-area) list drop-off notes for the big north-side campuses.

## Tell us how they get around

The nurse's answer to the third question matters more than it sounds. Someone who walked into the hospital may leave in a wheelchair, at least for the first week. If you're not sure, say "wheelchair" and we'll send a ramp van. A ramp van works for everyone. A regular van doesn't work for a chair.

## What happens at pickup

The driver comes to the entrance you told us. Hospital transport usually wheels the patient down in a hospital chair. The driver helps them into their own chair or into a seat in the van, secures everything, and you're on the road.

If you're riding along, tell us when you book so we save the seat.

## At home

The ride doesn't end at the curb. The driver helps your family member up the steps, through the door, and to a chair or bed. If you're not going to be home yet, tell us who will be.

## Things families forget

- **Prescriptions.** If the pharmacy stop is on the way, tell us. A short stop is fine.
- **Equipment.** A walker, a shower chair, oxygen. It all fits.
- **The cost.** We tell you the price when we confirm, and it doesn't change. See [pricing](/pricing).

## If the discharge slips

Hospitals change their minds. If tomorrow becomes the day after, one call moves the ride. No fee, no re-booking.
